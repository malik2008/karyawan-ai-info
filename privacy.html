Privacy Policy — Karyawan AI

Effective date: 21 November 2026. (Ringkasan Bahasa Indonesia di bagian bawah.)

1. Who uses this application

Karyawan AI is a personal automation tool operated by its owner. It is used only by the owner for the owner's own YouTube channel and is not offered as a service to the public.

2. YouTube API Services

Karyawan AI uses YouTube API Services. By using Karyawan AI you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access, collect and use

  • Karyawan AI requests only the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload.
  • It uses this access for one purpose: uploading videos created by the owner to the owner's own YouTube channel, together with the title, description and tags written for that video.
  • It does not read, collect or analyze any other YouTube or Google account data (no comments, subscribers, viewers, analytics, or data of other users).

4. Storage, sharing and cookies

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted as a secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the owner's automated job.
  • Karyawan AI does not sell, share or disclose user information to third parties. Finished videos are sent only to the platform chosen by the owner (YouTube) and, for review, to the owner's private Telegram chat. No YouTube account data is sent to Telegram.
  • Karyawan AI has no public website application and does not place cookies or tracking technologies on any user's device.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Generated video files are stored temporarily for at most 14 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Stored authorization data is deleted when access is revoked or the project is closed, and in any case within 30 days after a deletion request.
  • You can revoke Karyawan AI's access to your Google account at any time through the Google security settings page.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, contact the owner via github.com/malik2008.

6. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
