Karyawan AI

Karyawan AI is a personal automation tool that turns the owner's product photos and notes into short vertical videos (with AI-written narration and subtitles) and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel as Shorts.

This application uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Used only by its owner, for the owner's own channel — not a public service.
  • Requests only the youtube.upload permission.
  • Does not collect, sell or share data of other users.
